National Seminar • IRS Islamabad • Khair Publications

Ideology of Pakistan and the Role of Islamic Scholars

  • Date & Time: Wednesday, September 02, 2026 • 11:30 AM
  • Venue: Institute of Regional Studies (IRS), Ataturk Avenue, F-5/2, Islamabad
  • Organizers: Jointly organized by Institute of Regional Studies (IRS) & Khair Publications
  • Keynote Address: Sahibzada Muhammad Saeed (Founder, Khair Publications) on “Role of Sufia-e-Karam & Ideology of Pakistan”
  • Book Launch & Presentation: Newly translated English editions of “Sufism” and “Truthful Tales” formally presented

At the beginning of the seminar, Sahibzada Muhammad Saeed Bhorvi, founder of Khair Publications, addressed the gathering and highlighted the objectives of the seminar and the profound significance of its theme.

On this historic occasion, Allama Sahibzada Muhammad Masoom Bhorvi, together with distinguished religious scholars, academics, diplomats, and intellectuals, participated as special guests. The speakers shared their comprehensive perspectives on the intellectual foundation of Pakistan, the scholarly contributions of Sufi saints in the subcontinent, and the vital role of Ulema in fostering unity, moral reform, and spiritual resilience.

The News International • September 04, 2026

Role of Ulema, Sufis Vital to Understanding Pakistan’s Ideology

By Rasheed Khalid • Special Report

Prominent national coverage highlighting the key addresses delivered at the seminar organized jointly by the Institute of Regional Studies (IRS) and Khair Publications. The report documents how speakers emphasized that the spiritual traditions of Sufi saints and Muslim scholars form the intellectual cornerstone of Pakistan's ideological identity.

The article specifically features the formal launch of the newly published English translations of “Sufism” and “Truthful Tales” by Khair Publications, recognizing their contribution to introducing authentic Islamic thought to global English-speaking audiences.
